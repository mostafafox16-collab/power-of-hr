How the System Works

Four interconnected n8n workflows automating the entire HR lifecycle.

1

Intake & Validation

Validates PDF binary headers, prevents duplicate email/role submissions, uploads CVs to Google Drive, and logs rows in Google Sheets.

2

Hourly AI Screening

Extracts CV text (with Gemini OCR fallback for scanned PDFs), evaluates candidates via LLM rubrics, and sends interview or rejection emails.

3

RAG Policy Assistant

Splits HR policy documents into chunks, embeds them with Google Gemini into a Vector Store, and answers questions with source evidence.

4

CV Feedback Coach

Parses uploaded CVs against custom job descriptions and emails a structured 40-word summary, match score, and recommendation.

Inspect & Download Workflows

Sanitized n8n JSON blueprints (credentials, emails, and IDs stripped) ready for import.
